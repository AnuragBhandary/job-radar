We empower our people to stay resilient and relevant in a constantly changing world. We’re looking for people who are always searching for creative ways to grow and learn. People who want to make a real impact, now and in the future. Does that sound like you? Then it seems like you’d make a great addition to our vibrant international team.

We are looking for: Personal Assistant

You’ll make an impact by:

  • to support our team. This role is critical for ensuring smooth administrative processes and effective communication across global teams.
  • Execute request-to-approval workflows in MyMailSAP.
  • Handle service orders and hardware purchases in compliance with company policies.
  • Proactively manage status updates and ensure policy-compliant purchasing.
  • Coordinate travel arrangements, including itinerary planning and change handling.
  • Ensure compliance with company travel policies.
  • Manage travel expense reporting and reimbursement processes.
  • Manage cross-team scheduling and recurring meetings.
  • Handle rescheduling, conflict resolution, and availability checks.
  • Support structured planning for leadership and project meetings.
  • Communicate and coordinate with secretariats of other Siemens units for scheduling alignment.
  • Assist in preparing newsletters, presentations, and internal communications.
  • Coordinate with teams for content management (SharePoint, email, intranet).
  • Support visual storytelling and drafting strategic messages aligned with leadership guidance.

Use your skills to move the world forward!

  • Strong organizational and time-management skills.
  • Excellent communication skills in English (written and verbal).
  • Proficiency in MS Office tools and familiarity with SAP systems (MyMailSAP preferred).
  • Ability to work independently and take responsibility for assigned topics.
  • Experience in administrative support or similar roles is a plus.
  • Flexibility and adaptability to changing requirements and time zones.
  • Strong collaboration skills in virtual, global teams using tools like Microsoft Teams.
  • German language skills Must.
  • Experience working with or for German headquarters.
  • A dynamic and diverse team with a collaborative spirit.
  • A positive atmosphere across countries and cultures.
  • Opportunities to contribute to global IT transformation projects.
